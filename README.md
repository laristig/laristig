# Hi, I'm Laura

**Senior Product Manager — AI & Agentic Products** · Bogotá, Colombia

I lead the agentic vertical at a trade-finance fintech in Latin America. My team builds AI agents that operate at the business layer, so importers can move from one step of their operation to the next faster, whether that happens on WhatsApp, on the web platform, or on whatever interface comes next.

I own the full product cycle for these agents: vision, strategy, roadmap, discovery, validation, launch and continuous improvement. Day to day, that means turning user, business and technical needs into clear requirements, aligning stakeholders around priorities, and working closely with engineering, data, AI and marketing.

---

## How I think about agents

- **Flow first, agent second.** An agent only gets built when the workflow already exists and works on the platform. Automating a broken process just makes it fail faster.
- **Agents are interface-agnostic.** The agent lives in the operation layer. WhatsApp and the web app are surfaces, not the product.
- **Converse, then execute, then orchestrate.** Autonomy is earned in stages. Each level has to prove it is trustworthy before the next one is unlocked.
- **Reliability is a product requirement.** Accuracy thresholds are defined before anything is built, and a human stays in the loop wherever an error would be costly.
- **Every initiative is a contract with a number.** A hypothesis, the metric it should move, the threshold that kills it, and the date we read it. If the number does not move, we stop.
- **Adoption is the outcome, not shipping.** Success is measured by how many customers actually complete real tasks through an agent, not by what was released.

## How I work

I run initiatives through a five-station flow that keeps strategy and delivery connected:

```
Contract  ->  Technical gate  ->  Build  ->  Read  ->  Adoption
```

1. **Contract.** Prioritize the idea, map its assumptions, design cheap experiments, and write a short contract with the number that decides its fate.
2. **Technical gate.** Anticipate engineering questions, risks and feasibility before kickoff, and decide between a quick experiment and a larger build.
3. **Build.** Specify agent behavior with Spec-Driven Development, starting from a workflow that already exists, and keep every piece of work traceable back to its initiative.
4. **Read.** Compare the result against the contract's threshold. This is the step teams tend to skip, so I treat it as the constraint.
5. **Adoption.** Design the adoption intervention with marketing, and tell stakeholders what changed and what we learned, not just what we did.

## My AI-native PM operating system

I run my own product work on a multi-agent system built with Claude Code: 15 specialized subagents and 10 slash commands that chain them into end-to-end flows.

| Need | Agent |
|---|---|
| Prioritize, sequence and connect decisions to business goals | Product strategist |
| Map assumptions and design low-fidelity experiments | Experiment designer |
| Read a hypothesis against its contract's number | Hypothesis reader |
| Anticipate engineering objections and real feasibility | Agentic engineer |
| Check what is viable and what it costs on a messaging channel | Channel expert |
| React to an idea as a real customer would, persona by persona | Synthetic customer panel |
| Write the initiative contract or a full PRD | Contract / PRD writer |
| Decide what to tell each stakeholder, and in what order | Executive reporter |
| Turn a narrative into a branded deck or report | Deliverable designer |
| Register meetings, keep the knowledge base in sync, publish to Jira | Operations agents |

A few rules keep it honest:

- **No orchestrator agent.** I orchestrate. Agents advise, and I decide.
- **Most agents are read-only.** They produce judgment. Only a handful are allowed to write files or touch Jira, and they propose before they act.
- **Analysis is evidence.** When an agent's analysis supports a decision, it is saved and linked to that decision, so my calls rest on reasoning that can be reviewed rather than on intuition.
- **Three layers of knowledge.** History is append-only, current state gets rewritten instead of patched, and decisions live in their own log with what, when, who, why, and what would reverse them.
